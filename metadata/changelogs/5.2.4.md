# Animus 5.2.4

## Fixed

- The Fragment of Healing can no longer be moved, dropped, or pulled out by machines like the Aerial Interface. Creative is exempt and it still drops on death.
- The javelins, Sentient Shield, both bows and both scythes can now be enchanted.
- The Crystallized Spiritus Block shared a Hellfire Forge recipe with NeoVitae's Crystal Cluster, so only one was craftable. It is now cut from a Crystal Cluster on a stonecutter, either direction.
