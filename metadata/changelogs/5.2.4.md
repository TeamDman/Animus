# Animus 5.2.4

## Fixed

- The javelins, Sentient Shield, both bows and both scythes could not be enchanted at all. They now take the enchantments you would expect: the scythes count as swords, the javelins take trident enchantments like Impaling, Loyalty and Riptide, the bows take bow enchantments, and everything with durability accepts Unbreaking and Mending.
- The Crystallized Spiritus Block had the exact same Hellfire Forge recipe as NeoVitae's Crystal Cluster, so only one of the two could ever be made. The block is decorative now, so it is cut on a stonecutter from a Crystal Cluster instead, and can be cut back again if you want the other shape.
