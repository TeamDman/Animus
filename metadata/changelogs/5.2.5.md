# Animus 5.2.5

## Fixed

- The Unarmed Damage attribute now adds its value to bare-handed punches, no matter what granted it.
- The Sigil of the Monk's bonus unarmed damage was never applied, which also kept its execute, knockback, and Spiritus Snare effects from ever triggering. All of these now work, and the sigil's bonus shows on the Unarmed Damage attribute while it is active.
- The Runic Sentient Scythe and Hand of Death can now take Malum's Rebound and Ascension enchantments.
- The Runic Sentient Scythe now counts as a Malum scythe, which its tag file was meant to do but never did.
