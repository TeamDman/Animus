# Animus 5.2.14

## Fixed

- Malum's Rebound and Ascension enchantments now work on the Runic Sentient Scythe and Hand of Death.
- Hand of Death now counts as a Malum scythe.
- Pact of the Reaper no longer breaks the Runic Sentient Scythe or Hand of Death when sweeping a group.
- Malum scythe proficiency no longer applies twice to the Runic Sentient Scythe.
