# Animus 3.0.31

## Changes

### Bug Fixes

- Ritual of Arcane Mastery and Ritual of Floral Supremacy now work on dedicated servers. They were injected into Blood Magic's ritual registry on `ServerStartingEvent`, so the client never knew about them: the Ritual Diviner placed one rune per click instead of auto-building, activation showed "runes are wrong", and the ritual never rendered as active. Both now register during common setup on both sides.
- Ritual of Source Vitaeum no longer appears without Ars Nouveau installed. It was registered by annotation, so it was always present and simply did nothing. It now registers from the Ars Nouveau compat module.
- Codex Animus no longer shows the Magi, Manasteel Soul, Iron Heart and Soul Stained Blood entries when their mods are absent. The four entries were missing the mod flag that the other compatibility entries use.
- Codex Animus listed a Lapis Block for the Ritual of Clear Skies; it has always required Glowstone. The Ritual of Boundless Skies is fifteen minutes of flight, not ten. The Imperfect Ritual Stone entry now also lists the Rituals of Neptune's Blessing and the Warden.
- Imperfect ritual failures now report in chat instead of the action bar, where they were easy to miss. Failing to reach your Soul Network now reports itself instead of failing silently.
- Imperfect rituals no longer charge LP for a ritual that did not happen. The cost is now taken only after the effect succeeds.
- Imperfect rituals from other mods now say which integration is unavailable instead of claiming no ritual exists for the block.
