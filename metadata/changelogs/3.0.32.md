# Animus 3.0.32

## Changes

### Additions

- The Sentient Bow, Runic Sentient Scythe and Hand of Death now take on the colour of the Demon Will they are running on.

### Bug Fixes

- The Hand of Death's Execute no longer fires on bosses and packs can add their own bosses to the animus:disallow_execute entity tag. 
- Fixed a JEI error on startup for anyone playing without Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks.
