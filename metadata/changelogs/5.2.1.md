# Animus 5.2.1

## Added

- Ritual guidebook entries now open with an interactive multiblock render of the ritual's rune layout, including the Visualize button for in-world ghost placement
- The Animus Codex can now be found in JEI by searching "guide", "book", or "manual"
- Every ritual's EV upkeep and how often it ticks can now be set in the config, under a new "refresh" section. Rituals that charge per mob, per block or per item were already configurable and are unchanged.
- Added Simplified Chinese, contributed by BaoZouZac. Item, block and ritual names are translated; the Codex and the newest tooltips are still in English.

## Fixed

- Modonomicon entries for compatibility content now only show if the respective mod is loaded
- The `arcane_rune` is now optional in altar rune tags when Ars Nouveau is not present
- The Compatibility tab now hides itself entirely when none of those mods are installed
- The Animus advancements never appeared. None of them were being loaded, so the whole tree was missing from the advancement screen.
- Tier 6 Ascension is now earned by actually forming a Tier 6 Ara Vitae. Placing an ordinary altar no longer counts.
- The Rituals of Sol, Luna, Relentless Tides and Siphon stopped working once they had swept their area, and would only start again after a world reload. They now sweep continuously.
- The Ritual of Sol no longer places lights a block outside its range, and the Ritual of Luna no longer searches a different area to the one Sol fills. Both now cover the same ground, so Luna can collect everything Sol places.
- The Ritual of Nature's Leach could crash the game once enormous amounts of Spiritus Ruina built up around it.
- The Codex had the Ritual of Nature's Leach backwards. Spiritus Ruina makes it run faster, not slower, so it speeds itself up as it eats. Its effect range starts at 8 blocks and reaches 32 only with the Ritual Tinkerer.
- The Ritual of Source Vitaeum no longer appears when Ars Nouveau is not installed.
- The Runic Sentient Scythe now respects Malum's Necklace of the Narrow Edge and Necklace of the Hidden Blade. Wearing either stops it sweeping and gives it the single target damage the necklace promises.
