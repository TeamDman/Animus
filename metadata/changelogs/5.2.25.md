# Animus 5.2.25

## Added

- Added Iron's Arms 'n Artifice support with the Blood Bullet and Spirit Powder gun modifiers.
- Added the Vengeance Bullet gun modifier, which lets guns hurt EvilCraft's Vengeance Spirits (TeamDman/Animus#168).

## Changed

- Updated Simplified Chinese translations, contributed by ChuijkYahus (TeamDman/Animus#171).

## Fixed

- The Imperfect Ritual of Enhancement can no longer raise an enchantment more than one level past its maximum.
