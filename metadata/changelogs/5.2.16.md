# Animus 5.2.16

## Changed

- Source Attunement now gains more XP from spells with more glyphs.
- Arcane Channeling now gains more XP from rarer spells.

## Fixed

- Sentient Armor can gain the Source Attunement and Arcane Channeling upgrades again.
- Source Attunement level 5 now reduces mana cost by 20%.
- Arcane Channeling level 4 tooltip now shows its cooldown reduction.
- Sigil of the Phantom Chain no longer works on players.
