# Animus 5.2.8

## Added

- The Sentient Bow, Runic Sentient Scythe and Hand of Death now take on the colour of the Spiritus they are running on.

## Fixed

- The Hand of Death's Execute no longer fires on bosses and packs can add their own bosses to the animusnv:disallow_execute entity tag.
- Fixed a JEI error on startup for anyone playing without Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks.
