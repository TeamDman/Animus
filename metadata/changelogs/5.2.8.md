# Animus 5.2.8

## Added

- The Sentient Bow, Sentient Javelin, Runic Sentient Scythe and Hand of Death now take on the colour of the Spiritus they are running on.

## Fixed

- The Sentient Bow, Sentient Javelin and Sentient Shield always said they were attuned to Raw no matter which Spiritus you had.
- The Hand of Death's Execute no longer fires on bosses and packs can add their own bosses to the animusnv:disallow_execute entity tag.
