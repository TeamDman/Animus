# Animus 5.2.3

## Changes

- The Animus advancement tab now uses a deepslate bricks background instead of a stretched altar texture.

## Fixed

- The Sanguine Rectifier no longer keeps showing an orb after it has been taken out.
- The Sentient Shield could not be crafted because its recipe demanded more Spiritus than a Petty Spiritus Gem can hold. It now needs 64, a full Petty gem, like other sentient gear.
- Kills with the Runic Sentient Scythe and Hand of Death now drop Malum spirits. They previously never did despite the intended Malum integration.
- Fixed a server crash that could happen shortly after hitting a mob with the Runic Sentient Scythe or Hand of Death, when the game tried to save the snared mob.
- The Ritual of Sol now recognizes the Sigil of Blood Light and places Blood Light instead of ordinary torches when one is in its chest.
- The Sigil of Temporal Dominance now correctly detects blocks already being accelerated by Gadgets Against Grind or Time in a Bottle and refuses to stack with them.
- The Arcane Rune now stores up to 1,000 Source as documented, instead of 10,000.
- Blood-Infused Spellbook upgrades were demanding one orb rank higher than intended, making the final tier unreachable. Each tier now needs the orb the guide lists, and the final tier accepts the Divinus Orb of Vitae.
- Spellbook tooltips, JEI pages, and the guide book now show the correct Orb of Vitae names instead of outdated ones.
- The Codex Animus guide book now has a crafting recipe: a book plus a Tabula Rasa.
- The Transcendent Ascension advancement (formerly Tier 6 Ascension) could never be earned; it now unlocks when you build a Transcendent Ara Vitae.
- The Crystallized Spiritus Block is now presented as an alternate capstone for the Transcendent Ara Vitae, and the Transcendent Orb of Vitae as a higher-capacity alternative to the Divinus Orb, matching how altar tiers now work. Tooltips, JEI, advancements, and the guide book have been updated to match.
