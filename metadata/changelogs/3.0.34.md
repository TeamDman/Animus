# Animus 3.0.34

## Bug Fixes

- Fixed Sigil of Equivalency duplicating blocks when replacement materials run out.
- Equivalency now respects unbreakable blocks, Willful Stone ownership, and Forge break/place protection events.
- Fixed LP-powered spellcasting granting free mana and skipping LP payment.
- Fixed Ritual of Persistence leaving chunks forced after stopping or removal.
- Fixed Ritual of Serenity continuing to prevent spawns after stopping or removal.
- Fixed Ritual of Unmaking deleting enchantments when blank books run out.
- Fixed Unmaking ignoring enchanted books.
- Fixed blood wood stripping failing during startup.
