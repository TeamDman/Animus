# Animus 5.2.20

## Changed

- Sigil of the Demon Monk now keeps your empty hand empty: while it is active, items you pick up skip your selected hotbar slot if that slot is empty.
- Projectiles caught by the Sigil of the Demon Monk now go to your inventory instead of your empty hand.

## Fixed

- Fixed Sigil of the Demon Monk mining ores and other tool-gated blocks without dropping anything.
