# Animus 5.2.11

## Fixed

- Corrected several Simplified Chinese translations, contributed by ChuijkYahus.
- The Sigil of Equivalency no longer hands out drops when the replacement material is missing.
- The Sigil of the Phantom Builder keeps item data such as shulker box contents when placing blocks, and follows normal placement rules and protection.
- The Ritual of Unmaking leaves enchantments alone when there are not enough books nearby, and can pull books from more than one stack.
- The Ritual of Persistence releases its chunk loading when stopped, powered off, out of EV, or removed, and checks its saved chunk tickets when the world loads.
- The Ritual of Serenity stops blocking mob spawns once it is stopped, powered off, or removed.
- Rituals and sigils no longer confuse matching coordinates in different dimensions.
- The Sigil of the Free Soul returns you to your original position and dimension when the effect ends or you log out.
- The Sigil of Transposition teleports attacked creatures again, and checks for 5,000 EV before doing so.
- Anti-Life waits while its owner is away, respects block protection, and remembers its target block correctly after a restart.
- Blood Cores check the ground below the sapling spot when spreading trees.
- Sanguine Rectifier tank contents are saved reliably, including fluid piped in or out by other blocks.
