# Animus 3.0.6

## New Content

### New Imperfect Rituals

- **Ritual of Neptune's Blessing** (Prismarine Block, 2000 LP)
  - Grants Water Breathing and Dolphin's Grace for 15 minutes

- **Ritual of the Warden** (Sculk Block, 3000 LP)
  - Grants Obsidian Cloak for 15 minutes
  - Provides significant damage reduction

- **Ritual of the Manasteel Soul** (Manasteel Block, 2500 LP) - Requires Botania
  - Grants Emptiness and Speed for 15 minutes

- **Ritual of Soul Stained Blood** (Block of Hallowed Gold, 2000 LP) - Requires Malum
  - Grants Gaia's Bulwark for 15 minutes

- **Ritual of the Magi** (Source Gem Block, 2500 LP) - Requires Ars Nouveau
  - Grants Mana Regen for 15 minutes

- **Ritual of the Iron Heart** (Arcane Anvil, 3500 LP) - Requires Iron's Spellbooks
  - Grants Echoing Strikes III for 15 minutes

### JEI Integration

- Added comprehensive JEI support for all Imperfect Rituals
  - New "Imperfect Rituals" recipe category with custom display
  - Shows ritual name, LP cost, effect description, and activation instructions
  - Renders catalyst block over ritual stone for visual clarity
  - Click on Imperfect Ritual Stone to see all available rituals
  - Click on any catalyst block to see its specific ritual
  - Mod-dependent rituals only shown when the required mod is loaded

## Changes

### Artwork

- **Changed Transcendant Orb to make Bloodmagic orb styles**
- **New Artwork added for the Runic Sentient Scythe**
- **Model and Scaling adjustments to the Hand of Death Scythe**

### Balance

- **Standardized all effect-granting imperfect rituals to 15 minutes**
  - Boundless Skies increased from 10 minutes to 15 minutes
  - All other effect rituals already at 15 minutes

- **Runic Sentient Scythe rune bonuses increased by 35%**
  - Enhanced damage scaling from rune effects

- **Ritual of Clear Skies catalyst changed from Lapis Block to Glowstone**

### Runic Sentient Scythe

- Added dual-texture model support for better visual appearance

### Sigil of the Demon Monk

- Projectile catching now works while holding catchable projectiles (arrows, spectral arrows, tridents, firework rockets)
  - Previously required an empty main hand
  - Caught projectiles stack with held items of the same type
  - Different projectile types or full stacks go to inventory
  - Falls back to dropping at player's feet if inventory is full

## Bug Fixes

- Fixed Malum spirits not dropping correctly from Runic Sentient Scythe kills
- Fixed a potential crash if a villager converted to a zombie within the range of a Serenity Ritual
