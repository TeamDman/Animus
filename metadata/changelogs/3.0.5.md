# Animus 3.0.5

## Changes

### New Content
- **Sigil of the Demon Monk** - A curio sigil for unarmed combat
  - Equip in charm curio slot, toggle with right-click
  - +10 unarmed damage with knockback
  - Netherite + Efficiency V mining speed with bare hands
  - On kill: grants Resistance II (5s) and Regeneration I (2s)
  - Applies Soul Snare II (2s) on hit for guaranteed demon will drops
  - Demon will scaling: bonus damage equal to 1-15% of target max HP (scales with 1-4096 total will)
  - Execute mechanic: instant kill enemies below the damage threshold, rewards +200 LP, costs 5 will, spawns soul particles
  - Projectile catch: catch arrows, tridents, and firework rockets with empty main hand (cancels damage, gives item, costs 50 LP)
  - Fall damage immunity (costs 25 LP per fall)
  - Costs 5 LP per hit or block mined
  - Works when placed in Sigil of Holding
  - Crafted via alchemy array: Reagent of the Fist + Reinforced Slate
  - Reagent of the Fist crafted in alchemy table: Rotten Flesh + Diamond Pickaxe + Iron Shovel + Iron Axe
- Added new sound effects: `ninja_time` (sigil activation) and `execute` (execute kills)

### Recipes
- Key of Binding now crafted in Tier 1 Blood Altar with 1500 LP (was alchemy array with Simple Key + Reagent: Binding)

### Rituals
- Ritual of Unmaking now ignores items enhanced by the Imperfect Ritual of Enhancement (configurable via `unmakingDisallowEnhanced`, default: true)
- **Ritual of Culling** now supports player-like kills when raw demon will is available
  - When raw demon will is present in the area, mobs are killed as if the ritual owner killed them
  - This enables player-only drops like blaze rods from blazes
  - Looting bonus based on other will types: each type (corrosive, destructive, vengeful, steadfast) with >10 will adds +1 looting level
  - 10% chance to consume 1 raw will per kill (configurable via `cullingWillConsumeChance`)
  - Thorns damage from mobs is blocked when using FakePlayer kills
  - Configurable via `cullingPlayerKillDrops` (default: true)
- **Ritual of Endless Greed** - A new ritual that collects mob drops and XP
  - When mobs die within a 15x15x5 area, their drops are transferred to a container on top of the ritual stone
  - If the container is full or missing, items are destroyed
  - Experience orbs are automatically collected and stored in the first Tome of Peritia found in the container
  - Tome slot cached for performance (refreshes every 5 seconds)
  - Very low LP upkeep (5 LP per second, 1 LP per item collected by default)
  - Flat rune configuration using only basic runes (no dusk runes required)
  - Activation cost: 5000 LP
